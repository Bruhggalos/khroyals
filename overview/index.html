KH Royals: the Kingdom Heights school app

One app for families, students and staff: the school's site and news, the daily Planner, a photo gallery, Royals Connect groups, car line pickup, emergency alerts, activities, forms and the office's admin tools. Android, iPhone, iPad and the web, with one secure backend.

Who uses it · 6 kinds of users

Everyone signs in with their email and a 6-digit code: no passwords. New accounts wait for the office to approve them, and each role sees only what it should.

What it costs to run

Hosting for the app's backend (accounts, database, files and photos, live updates, notifications and email), two ways: on Supabase, where it runs today, and the same setup built on AWS. Slide to change the number of people; every line recalculates. List prices in US dollars, checked October 2026.
